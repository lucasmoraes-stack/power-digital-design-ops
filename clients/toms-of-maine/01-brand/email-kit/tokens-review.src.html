Image or live text

What the export slices show in the 6 Figma emails. This describes those 6, not a rule: the 20 sent emails in the inbox folder use other hero types too, and some may carry live text.

  • Hero in the 6 Figma emails: one image slice with the New Kansas headline, Rubik subtitle and CTA inside.
  • Hero in the 20 sent emails: also text on flat or gradient teal with a side photo, text on a gradient with products below, price-led “20% OFF” heroes, and full photos with text on top. When the hero goes live text is an open item.
  • Band heading, intro, product name, card body, Rubik labels and buttons ship as HTML.
  • The whole footer ships as HTML, in Gotham Bold, with Salesforce Marketing Cloud AMPscript in the copyright line.

Color

Open items

What is left before the status moves from draft to approved.

  • Live-text heroIn which situations does the hero ship as live text instead of a single image slice?
  • Blue backgroundsSeen in Relaxation Day, Labor Day Savings End Tonight and Stay Fresh, Naturally Confident. Hex values and when they are allowed (guideline v4.3 deprecates the old Sky).
  • Resolved · New Kansas filesAll 15 .otf files are in 01-brand/identity/fonts/ (out of git, licensed). Still open for email: whether the license covers a hosted webfont, or live text falls back to Georgia.
  • Gotham in header and footerKeep it as in the approved emails, or switch to Rubik, the guideline's functional face?
  • First-name field in SFMCNo approved email uses personalization. Which AMPscript attribute?
  • Footer reason lineThe approved emails have no “you're receiving this because…” line. Keep it that way?
  • Mobile and dark modeMobile sizes (band heading 32/34) and the dark palette derived from #05453D are proposals, not from the Figma.
  • White → #0CAB97 gradientWhite text at the end of this gradient is 2.88:1. Kit rule: white text never sits in that range.
