PRODUCTION RULES LEARNED (v0.6) · 2026-10-07 · REVIEW ONLY, NOT A MODULE. From the November 2026 batch (03-work/email/2026-11-broadcasts: brief.md rounds 2 and 3, qa-r1.md, qa-r2.md). Every module, old and new, is built and checked against these; the README section “Aprendizados de novembro (v0.6)” carries the same list in PT-BR
R1 · SEAMS · every composite baked on a band tile (product group, cut-out, system shot, collage) starts at the tile row and column where it sits in the 600px layout (measured in Chrome, never guessed: 01 hero was 251, not 235) and ALL baked lighting (light pool, darker floor, floor shadow, tear shadow) is multiplied by a border window that reaches ZERO over the last 40px of each border (compose_assets.py border_window), so the image edges are the untouched tile. Measure, do not eyeball: seam_check (file border vs tile) and the step in the 680 render on strips with no product and no text, both 1.5 levels or less. November 01 shipped a 21-level line across the hero before this rule
R2 · PRODUCT TITLES · the last two words of every product title are held together (&nbsp; or a white-space:nowrap span), in cards, E27 boxes, rows and stacks, desktop and mobile. Headlines keep the rule from rules.md (no widow); a serif word that would sit alone is split in its own line pair (SIT / ALL DAY)
R3 · 2-UP CARD ROWS · title cell with a FIXED height = the longest title of the grid (80px = 4 lines of 20px; 3 lines = 60px broke row 3 in November), description cell fixed (84px = 4 lines of 21px), then price + button in a third cell with valign=bottom: prices and buttons sit on the same line across both columns in every row. Mobile releases the heights (height:auto). Check the longest title at 258px before choosing the height (E40)
R4 · ORANGE ON MAJOR BROWN · an orange title over Major Brown or the halftone is 19px 800 (large text) on desktop AND mobile. A mobile size override must be scoped to the Tap Shoe and Patriot bands only (class per band, E41 .e41-t-sm); a global .title{17px} on mobile broke this in November 05
R5 · NO PALETTE PNG · never an 8-bit (P mode) PNG: camo posterizes and soft shadows band. A cut-out on a flat or textured band is a JPG baked on that band (and a -dm twin when the band is light paper); PNG-24 only where real transparency is needed and the file stays light. v0.6 rewrote three kit packshots as PNG-24; four are still palette (README, open item)
R6 · TEAR CRUMBS · crumbs along a torn edge stay fine: radius 0.6 to 1.3px at 2x, 0.5 to 4.5px from the line (the rev-oct-03 grain). No 3 to 5px specks 10 to 25px above the line. v0.6 rebuilt every textured E18 edge of the kit this way (tools/compose_v06.py edges); the tears baked inside E21, E24, E24B, E26, E30 and E33 still carry the old dots (open item)
R7 · DETAIL CIRCLES · a detail circle only when the feature reads at about 40% of the circle (a zipper pull, a pocket edge, a pass-through flap). In camo a catalogue crop reads as fabric texture only: then use the whole system shot with live-text labels and orange leader lines (E38), and ask the client for the original detail photos
R8 · PHOTO HEROES AND PHOTO TRANSITIONS (owner decision 2026-10-08, replaces the 120 to 160px melt) · no alpha fade and no blur melt between a photo and a band: every photo edge that meets a band is a torn edge or another organic texture (the photo as a ripped print on the band, white paper core along the rip, cast shadow from the batch light; or the next band's sheet torn over the photo, E18 baked, fine crumbs R6). A soft darkening INSIDE the photo, under the live text, is allowed (not a transition), only as much as AA needs. Full-bleed photo hero: the photo covers the band edge to edge down to the torn edge, live text over it (background image + VML + bgcolor). When background-size:cover cuts a face or puts the logo on a cap at 375px, the hero gets its own mobile crop (swapped by a media rule, saved at 2x of the mobile css size, not more), same rule: torn, never faded
R9 · STUDIO HERO · a packshot group opens an email (E35) only when no in-use photo of that line exists in the bank or the store images; record that in the brief. The kit default is still an in-use photo hero
R10 · NO REPEATS · no image appears twice in the same batch (November 05 first reused the 02 gift card and the 02 angler); each email of a batch also opens with a different hero treatment and no block layout repeats
R11 · COLLAGE FRAMES · a framed photo near a baked tear either clears it (frame ends 30px or more above the torn line) or crosses it decisively (40px or more past it). A frame clipped by 15px reads as a mistake (E36)
R12 · CLIENT COPY · client copy is placed literally: headline, subheadline, body, product names, lines, CTA, order. Only the kit’s typography changes (real caps, curly apostrophe, held words, two-voice line split). Anything suspicious (a CTA that does not match, a price outside its tier, a duplicated subheadline, a double period) goes to the brief’s list for the client, never silently fixed
R13 · REFERENCES · reference emails give structure only (order, rhythm, device). Never their colour, type, copy, badges or swatches
R14 · WEIGHT · about 800 KB of images per email, light mode, counting every composite at its real size; -dm twins are counted separately (some clients fetch them). At most 4 different textures and one heavy composite (over 100 KB) besides the hero; a mobile-only hero counts for mobile
R15 · QA GATE · lint_layout.py (03-work/email/2026-11-broadcasts: width, image, edge, photo, margin, text, clip checks at 600 and 375) runs clean, then the Email QA Reviewer does a visual pass on the 680 / 375 / dark renders, BEFORE the owner sees any layout. Lint never sees image defects (seams, crumbs, crops): those are the reviewer’s
