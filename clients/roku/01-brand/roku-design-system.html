Imagery · RKU26013

A real room, an illustrated city outside

Mixed media, as the concept deck's references do, in our own composition: a bright, real apartment living room whose floor-to-ceiling windows look out on a pure flat illustration of the city. The room and the TV ad are generated with Figma AI. The city is our own illustration (sky, sun, clouds, three depths of towers, lit windows), keyed into the glass only, so frames, plants and furniture stay in front of it. It is a view outside, never a pattern on the wall.

Do

  • Real room: warm white walls, a wall of black-framed windows, light oak console, cream boucle seating, lavender textiles, a plant, soft daylight, symmetrical front-on camera.
  • TV switched off in the plate, then the ad composited onto the glass: purple set, one product, copy on the left third.
  • The city is a pure illustration: flat fills, crisp edges, no photo texture, no blending with the room. Three lilac depths, a warm sun, flat clouds, a few lit windows. The same city in every industry.
  • One living cue per industry in the room (the dog for Pet) and purple props on the coffee table.
  • Plates are generated with blown-out white glass so the illustration keys in cleanly, edges included. Keep the wall above the windows empty for the headline.

Don't

  • Moody, dark rooms like RKU26008, or a purple cast over the whole photo.
  • Claymation or 3D-toy looks (the vibe.co problem).
  • Roku City artwork, brand illustration library pieces or brand icons.
  • Readable brand names or fake logos inside generated images; on-screen copy is set in type.
  • City printed on the wall like wallpaper, or tinted into the photo. It lives behind the glass.
  • Copying the deck reference layout: this is a new version, with its own camera, furniture and type placement.

Layout

Lockup, headline, TV, room, CTA

One top block on the wall: lockup, headline, then the Learn more button, all centered. The window glass starts right under it and the TV sits just below the glass top: the empty sky band above the TV is cut from the plate so text and TV read as one unit. Room cues fill the foreground corners.

"Learn more" with a chevron, Roku Display Bold, white on Primary Purple, radius 0.35 × type size, soft Midnight shadow. Part of the top block, centered under the headline, never over the TV or the window.

Motion

Headline and screen move, the room breathes

From the brief: animation is mainly the headline and the TV screen, with subtle keyframes in the foreground or background for quality.

Headline

The industry word swaps in place: old word slides up and out, new word rises in. Line two stays still.

TV screen

Roku wordmark fades up, the line types on, the progress bar runs. The screen glow pulses once on each swap.

Ambient

City windows blink at random, lamp light breathes, the plant sways a few degrees, the skyline drifts a little for parallax.

Timing proposal until length is confirmed: 3 seconds per industry, so five industries make a 15-second CTV spot with an end hold on the lockup.

Build handoff

How a frame is made and named

  1. Generate the room plate (white blown-out window glass, TV off, empty wall, no text) and the TV ad with Figma AI into img/raw/ of clients/roku/03-work/ads/2026-rku26013-sell-it-on-roku/.
  2. Run compose.py (illustrated city keyed into the window glass, ad on the TV, wall extended), then build.py (headline, lockup and on-screen copy in HTML, exported by Chrome).
  3. Check every frame: lockup and headline inside the safe area, nothing on the platform bands, headline size equal across industries, no white fringe around plants, fur or the TV.
  4. Export PNG (static) and MP4 (animated) to 04-deliverables/ads/2026-rku26013-sell-it-on-roku/.
  5. Name files RKU26013-SellItOnRoku-1440x1440-Static-Meta-Pet-V1.png; CTV is RKU26013-SellItOnRoku-1920x1080-Animated-CTV-AllIndustries-V1.mp4.
  6. Add the job to the Roku Deliverables Hub and republish it from a fresh read.
