# November spacing and layout rules (Lucas review, 2026-10-16)

Measured against the October finals. Both builders use these; 4:5 canvas 1080x1350, Story 1080x1920.

## Spacing scale (8px grid)
- Side margins 80. Top margin 80 (logo top). Story: 250 top / 250 bottom safe, 120 sides for text.
- Logo to headline: 40. Headline to body: 20. Body to visual: 56. Visual to closing line: 48.
- Card padding: 40 top/bottom, 44 sides. Inside a card: label to body 10, body line-height 1.3.
- Stacked cards or list rows: 28 gap between items; numbers/icons align on one vertical axis; text left edges align.
- A card never touches another card or the frame edge by accident: either overlap deliberately (>= 40px) or keep >= 28px apart.

## Composition
- No empty areas without a job. Every frame: copy block + one visual that fills the remaining height (photo, scene or UI), anchored to an edge or the centre. If there is a gap > 120px that holds nothing, the visual is too small or misplaced.
- PNG cutouts sit centred on their shape (optical centre), scaled to 70 to 80% of the shape, whole.
- Icon groups need hierarchy: one primary element (bigger, Pink), the rest secondary (smaller, Dark Blue or White), aligned on a grid or inside one card. Never scatter loose icon discs along an edge.
- Decoration must belong to the idea; remove "random" extras (a lone product in a corner).
