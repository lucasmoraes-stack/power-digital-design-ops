## What the October finals changed (Lucas, Figma "kat os", read 2026-10-16)

November follows the finals, not the September v2 page:
- Headlines in **sentence case**, one key word or phrase in **Pink** ("Start with what you **need**", "Pay **over time**"). A closing period on cover statements is fine ("Lease-to-own, explained.").
- **Logo** only on the cover and on the closing slide (top left on covers, centered at the foot on statement closers). Not on middle slides.
- **No CTA pill, no disclaimer** on organic art (the client copy has no CTA on art; the disclaimer lives in the caption).
- **Line accent is back:** a thick Pink line that turns a rounded corner and continues as a row of Pink dots, entering from a frame edge, used once per frame behind the visual.
- Cards: solid White, rounded, with a Dark Blue or Pink offset plate. Icons are line icons in Pink discs or on White. Product cutouts sit in White tiles or on flat Light Blue / Cream / Pink shapes.
- Photos are real people, full bleed or in large rounded cards; copy over a photo only inside a White card.
