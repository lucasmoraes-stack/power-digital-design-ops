## 2. Copy issues for the Copywriter

Keep the client's meaning. Fix, don't rewrite from scratch. Operation-wide rule: no em or en dashes anywhere in copy or captions. Scan result: the client draft has no em or en dashes today; keep it that way in every edit and in captions.

Global issues (apply to all posts):
- **Case (question for the client, do not decide alone):** the draft is in Title Case. The PDF guideline does not mandate case; the approved social design system says sentence case for headlines. Confirm with the account lead before converting.
- **Punctuation consistency:** mixed straight and curly apostrophes (e.g. 05 "you're" straight, 04 "Doesn’t" curly). Pick one.
- **Credit-score repetition:** headlines built on "credit score" in 02 s4, 06 s3, 13 s1, 15 s1, plus "alternative approval criteria" in the body of 01, 02, 03, 06, 13, 15. Katapult learning (2026-09-09): don't let credit score carry the set; move it to subhead level where possible and lead with the concrete purchase.
- **Jargon, off-voice:** "alternative approval criteria", "lease-purchase agreement", "structures work differently", "evaluating applications", "retail ecosystem". Voice is talking to a friend. Keep legally required terms, simplify the rest.
- **Third person:** most lines say "Katapult gives shoppers". Brand voice speaks as "we" to "you". Check per line where legal allows.
- **Length vs type scale:** 4:5 headline is 88px (about 20 to 23 characters per line on the 920px safe width), body 32px "short lines only" (about 55 characters per line). 9:16 headline 96px (about 17 characters per line), display 104px only for four words or fewer. Titles over 50 characters or bodies over 110 characters are flagged below.
- **Carousel CTA:** no carousel has a CTA. House CTA is "Leap Forward." Question for the client whether to add a closing slide (N7).
- **Legal, lease-purchase context:** any line saying or implying speed, ownership, approval, "pay over time", number of retailers, or comparisons with credit needs client legal review. Katapult is not credit. Never promise approval.
