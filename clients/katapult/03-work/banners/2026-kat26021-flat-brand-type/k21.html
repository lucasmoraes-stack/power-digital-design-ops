KAT26021 · PMAX and Google programmatic · MOF/BOF · Cyber Week

Flat Brand Type Cyber

No imagery. A full-bleed brand-color ground (Dark Blue on V1, Cream on V2) and an oversized type stack that shouts the deal, so the banner reads before anything else in the feed does. Subhead and pink CTA underneath turn the urgency into one next step.

  • 8 sizes
  • 2 iterations
  • 16 deliverables
  • V1/V2 static
Headline
The deals won't wait. And now you don't have to.
Subhead
Lease-to-own on thousands of Cyber Week deals.
CTA
Shop Cyber Deals Now

Copy is the client's, placed exactly as in the approved Figma. The headline is only broken into lines, with each sentence starting on its own line. Subhead on the PMAX sizes, 300x600 and 160x600; 300x250, 728x90 and 320x50 carry headline and CTA only. No disclaimer, because the brief's copy has none.

Color is the whole test. V1: Dark Blue ground, first sentence White, second Pink, "deals" and "now" plus the subhead in Lavender. V2: Cream ground, first sentence Blue, second Pink, "deals" and "now" plus the subhead in Dark Blue. The CTA stays the brand's pink pill with white text on both grounds.

Type is Aktiv Grotesk: Bold headline at -3% tracking, Regular subhead, Medium CTA. Every size follows the Figma frame's own line breaks, sizes and positions. The tall skyscrapers center the whole stack.

Safe zones: 5% inset on PMAX, 4 to 12px on programmatic. Copy, logo and CTA sit fully inside.
