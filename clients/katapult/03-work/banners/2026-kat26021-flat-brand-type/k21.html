Copy is the client's, placed exactly as written. The headline is only broken into lines, with each sentence starting on its own line. Subhead on the PMAX sizes and 300x600; 160x600, 300x250, 728x90 and 320x50 carry headline and CTA only, the Katapult banner default. No disclaimer, because the brief's copy has none.

Color is the whole test: Dark Blue ground, first sentence in White, second in Pink, subhead in Light Blue. The ground is Dark Blue so the CTA can stay the brand's pink pill with white text.

Type is Aktiv Grotesk Bold with tight leading. Each size picks its own line breaks and the headline is scaled to fill the space between the logo and the subhead.

Safe zones: 5% inset on PMAX, 4 to 12px on programmatic. Copy, logo and CTA sit fully inside.
