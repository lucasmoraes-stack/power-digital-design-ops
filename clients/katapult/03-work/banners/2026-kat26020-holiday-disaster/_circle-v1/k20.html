Holiday Disaster

A small holiday mishap, cropped into a circle on the Dark Blue field so the type never sits on the photo. The headline sets up in White and lands the joke in Pink, and the pink CTA turns the panic into the fix. V1 kitchen, V2 living room, same layout per size so only the imagery and copy change.

For review. All 16 built. Open questions for the account lead are in the production notes: the disclaimer (from the Figma frames, not the brief) and its sizes.

Images are the two the client likes, not regenerated. Only lightened, as the brief asks: midtones and shadows lifted, a touch of contrast put back so the flat Eggleston look doesn't go hazy, and the orange cast trimmed so the whites read white. Nothing in the pictures was changed.

Framing: per the creative direction, the photo is cropped into a circle on a color field instead of carrying type. The circle always bleeds off at least one edge. Big circles show the whole mishap with the room around it; small ones (300x600 and down) crop in tight on the turkey or the tree. A Light Blue ring echoes the Bounce: dotted on the left, solid on the right.

Type: Aktiv Grotesk Bold, tight leading. First sentence White, second Pink: the color break is the punchline. Each size uses the largest headline that fits both versions, so V1 and V2 match in size and position.

Copy is the client's, verbatim, only broken into lines. V2's CTA has no period, as in the brief. CTA is the brand pink pill with white text.

Disclaimer: not in the brief's copy, but the account lead placed one in the Figma 4x5 frames ("Lease-purchase service. Total cost exceeds cash price. Approval required. Not available in MN, NJ, WI, WY. See katapult.com."). Carried verbatim on the three PMAX sizes. Left off programmatic, where there's no legible room and earlier Katapult jobs ran without it. To confirm with the account lead.

Safe zones: 5% inset on PMAX, 4 to 12px on programmatic. Logo, copy and CTA sit inside and clear of the circle.
