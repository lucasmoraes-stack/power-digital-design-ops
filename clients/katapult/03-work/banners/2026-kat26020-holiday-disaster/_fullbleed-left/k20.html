For review. All 16 built. Open questions for the account lead are in the production notes: the disclaimer (from the Figma frames, not the brief) and its sizes.

Images are the two the client likes, not regenerated. Only lightened, as the brief asks: midtones and shadows lifted, a touch of contrast put back so the flat Eggleston look doesn't go hazy, and the orange cast trimmed so the whites read white. Nothing in the pictures was changed.

Framing (round 2, full-bleed): the photo fills the whole frame, cropped around the mishap so the turkey or the tree always lands mid-frame, clear of the type. The headline never sits loose on the picture: each line is a solid strip (White strip with Dark Blue type, then Dark Blue strip with Pink type), so it reads on any part of the photo. The photo fades into solid Dark Blue at the bottom, where the CTA, disclaimer and logo sit; the logo is never on the photo. On 728x90 and 320x50 the photo takes the right end of the banner and fades into the Dark Blue that carries logo, headline and CTA. Round 1 (photo in a circle on Dark Blue) was replaced in internal review.

Type: Aktiv Grotesk Bold, tight leading. First sentence on White, second in Pink: the color break is the punchline. Each size uses the largest headline that fits both versions, so V1 and V2 match in size and position.

Copy is the client's, verbatim, only broken into lines. V2's CTA has no period, as in the brief. CTA is the brand pink pill with white text.

Disclaimer: not in the brief's copy, but the account lead placed one in the Figma 4x5 frames ("Lease-purchase service. Total cost exceeds cash price. Approval required. Not available in MN, NJ, WI, WY. See katapult.com."). Carried verbatim on the three PMAX sizes. Left off programmatic, where there's no legible room and earlier Katapult jobs ran without it. To confirm with the account lead.

Safe zones: 5% inset on PMAX, 4 to 12px on programmatic. Checked by script on every frame: logo, copy and CTA inside the safe zone, nothing overlapping, nothing covering the mishap, and logo, CTA and disclaimer on the solid part of the fade.
