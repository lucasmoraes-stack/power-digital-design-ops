Production notes

Images: eight products generated with AI as top-down shots on white (sofa, TV, tire, refrigerator, bed, laptop, washing machine, game console), then silhouetted with their own contact shadow kept, so they sit on the ground instead of floating. No brand, logo or text on any product.

Arrangement: rows of products, each row sharing one height and running edge to edge across its area. The subhead's three nouns (couch, TV, tire) lead wherever there's room: 8 products on PMAX and 300x600, 4 on 160x600 and 300x250, 3 on 728x90, the sofa alone on 320x50.

Ground: very light salmon #F6E6DE, with a fine paper grain and a soft lift at top left from the same key light the shadows fall away from.
