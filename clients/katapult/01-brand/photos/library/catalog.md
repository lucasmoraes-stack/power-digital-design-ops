# Katapult · Image library catalogue

The single index of usable photos. `build_image_library.py` reads the table below; each row is one tile on the Image Library page.

- **File**: path relative to `clients/katapult/01-brand/photos/`. Originals stay full resolution, outside git.
- **Collection**: `stock` (Adobe Stock, received 2026-10-13 from `Desktop/kat images`; the duplicate `AdobeStock_236965719 (1)` was dropped); `ai` (generated with Figma AI, 960 x 1280).
- **Source**: the Adobe Stock ID, to find the licence; for `ai`, the generator and model.
- **Category**: phone, laptop (laptop or tablet), together (couples, families, friends), at-home, unboxing, retail (merchants, tires and auto), room (no people).
- **Text space**: where live copy can sit on the photo.
- **Brand fit** against the guideline: `on-brand`; `with care` (eyes closed, dim, busy or face not visible); `no people` (background or product context only, never the main image).
- **Rights**: `licensed`, `to confirm`, or `generated` (AI, no licence needed).
