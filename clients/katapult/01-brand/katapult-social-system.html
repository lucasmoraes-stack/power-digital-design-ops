Clear space: the logo’s x-height on every side (the marked band, top of the u to the baseline: 66.5 of the artwork’s 116 units, 12.8% of its width), free of copy, shapes and edges. At 250px wide that is 32px. --kp-logo-cs holds the value and the post layouts top up their gap with it.

Placement: near the headline, top or directly under the copy block, or centered on a solid White card. Never over photography or a busy image, never in running text.

96px, the digital minimumShown at actual size. Nothing smaller, in any format.
250px, the 4:5 post size--kp-logo-w: 240px on 1:1, 250px on 4:5, 280px on 9:16, all on a 1080px canvas, so a phone feed never shows it under 96px.

Do Logo use

  • Use Pink whenever the background allows it.
  • Place it on solid primary colors with enough contrast.
  • Keep it horizontal and at its own proportions, 521 : 116. Set the width, never the height.
  • Co-branding: logos side by side, equal visual size, middle aligned, clear space kept.

Don’t Logo use

  • Place it on a photo or a busy image.
  • Stretch, skew, rotate, outline, crop or add effects.
  • Rebuild it in Aktiv Grotesk or any font, or have AI render it.
  • Swap in the K icon for the logo in marketing posts.
  • Set it inside running text.
Graphic language · updated 2026-09-28

Shapes and interaction, no line graphics

The Bounce line graphics are retired: no Super graphic, no dotted and solid support arcs, no carousel seam arcs, no dotted swipe arrow. Katapult does not use them in current creative, and the June 2022 guideline is outdated on this point (account lead review, 2026-09-28). Forward motion now comes from composition: stepping tiles, progress squares, cards that stack and fan, and color blocks that change slide to slide.

The v2 components rebuild the client's engaging-format references in Katapult's own palette and flat shapes: UI input cards, product pickers, bingo and tic-tac-toe grids, a Venn, a triangle, board-game tracks, checklists, calendars, icon grids, photos with a floating UI card and color-blocked panels. They carry no words of their own. Fields are empty shapes, diagram corners hold icons, and every string on a post is client copy.

Slide skeleton

.kp-v2 on the article, then .kp-bg for full-bleed layers and .kp-lay for the safe area: optional .kp-track and logo, .kp-copy, and .kp-stage, which fills the room the copy leaves. Stage pieces are placed in cqw/cqh, so a composition rescales to any copy length. .bl-b, .bl-t, .bl-x and .bl-r let a stage bleed off an edge.

Text on images

Never on a photo. Copy that has to sit over a photo goes in a solid White .kp-ui card, in Dark Blue. The card's flat offset plate (.plate-*) gives depth without a shadow or a tint. The logo never sits on a photo.

New grounds

.kp-theme-blue: White text on Blue (7.55 : 1). .kp-theme-orange: Dark Blue text on Orange (6.13 : 1). Light Blue and Orange appear otherwise only as shapes, photo backgrounds and icon discs, never behind text.

Icon discs

.kp-ico in the approved icon pairings: t-w, t-s, t-c (Dark Blue icon, Pink accent), t-p (White icon on Pink), t-db (Light Blue icon, Pink accent). The dashed ring marks where the supplied line icon goes.

Photography and iconography

Real people, uplifted

Consumers, individuals and families, celebrating and connecting around the things Katapult helps them buy. People are the focal point and look like real people, of many ages and backgrounds.

Do Photography

  • Individual portraits: one subject on a plain, solid background that complements the palette.
  • Authentic, natural, fun, well lit.
  • Touches of color that echo the palette.
  • Leave a clean area for the headline; re-crop the same photo before moving the copy.
  • Crop app screens and product grids tight enough that their content is readable.
Cream Light Blue Pink Lightest Grey

Don’t Photography

  • Images without people (product cutouts in the product row are the exception).
  • Cropped or cut-off faces.
  • Annoyed, upset or moody expressions.
  • Black and white, filters, heavy color grading.
  • Low contrast, too dark, too light, blown out.
  • Textured or patterned backgrounds behind a portrait.

Carousel and Story patterns

Nine patterns for the Q4 organic run

Same tokens, ratio classes, themes and safe zones as the templates above. Carousel slides are 4:5 (1080 × 1350) and a carousel may alternate or color-block themes when it serves the rhythm, as long as the post keeps one stated color rule. The Story poll is 9:16 with the Stories safe area, 250 top and 250 bottom. Live components at 30%; gray blocks are labeled image slots.

Fit tests use client copy exactly as written, chosen from the longest lines in the run. Headlines up to about 63 characters sit in four lines at 88px, subtexts up to about 190 characters in four or five lines. No case transform runs on headlines, subtexts or options, so every line keeps the case it was typed in.

No line graphics

Seams, support arcs, the Super graphic and the swipe arrow are retired (2026-09-28). Patterns N1 to N9 below keep their layouts without them; new work builds on the v2 components in the Shapes section.

Disclaimer is optional

Not required and not placed in the 15 posts. Every pattern keeps room for .kp-disclaimer at the bottom safe line; fit tests that show it are for reference only.

New sizes approved

Brand Guardian, 2026-09-28. Body 36px on 4:5 and 38px on 9:16, Light (templates stay at 32px). Step numeral 200px, stat number 300px, prompt line 66px, all Medium. Display 104px extended to 4:5 covers of four words or fewer.

Voice quick reference

Talk like a friend, not a salesperson

Headlines invite, they don’t tell the whole story. Short sentences, simple words, one playful moment per post at most. Read it aloud before it ships. No em or en dashes in any copy.

Down-to-earth and direct
We can help you get that. A personalized plan you can trust that also trusts you. We do the hard work to keep things simple. The oh-so-simple application.
Engaging and friendly
Your path from ‘maybe’ to ‘all mine.’ On your mark, set, shop. We treat human beings like, well, human beings. We make integration a snap.
Aspirational and positive
You’re more than a credit score. We’re AI with dignity. Fintech with heart. Work for a company where humanity is a required skill. It’s a little good karma for commerce.
